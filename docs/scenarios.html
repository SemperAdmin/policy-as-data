Scenarios

Three fixed cases run through the rules encoded from MARADMIN 051/23, Expansion of the Marine Corps Military Parental Leave Program. Every line names the paragraph it rests on. A line that rests on a value no person has yet confirmed against the issuing authority's copy is withheld and says which value. This is a demonstration of cited computation, not a calculator and not a determination of anyone's entitlement.

Rules: 2 of 5 encoded values confirmed by a person; lines resting on the minimum increment, the maximum number of increments, the merge window are withheld. The source paragraph for every value is on the message's page; the confirmation state of each is on the verification queue.

One verifier is available. Since 2026-08-08 a value is admitted on 1 reading rather than the 2 the policy requires. No figure on this page is an entitlement determination, and none reaches a Marine under this deviation.

What the engine refuses, by name

Questions the encoded rules cannot answer are refused with the reason, never estimated.

  • Convalescent leave - requires a health care provider recommendation and commander approval (para 7.a.(1)(a)); not computable from encoded rules.
  • Window extension under para 8.a.(3) - a commander determination against six qualifying conditions; the engine reports the unextended date only.
  • Reserve Component forfeiture particulars (para 8.c.(4)) - not yet encoded in rules.json.
