Generalized Orders Administrative Tools programme

7 rendered publications with status, supersession, and citation anchors - every claim next to the authority that backs it. Cancelled policy remains available for historical reference and is labeled.

Five authority chains trace a policy from the message a Marine reads up to the statute or Department of Defense instruction it rests on. They are drawn from 54 documents and 351 cited references. A link exists only where the issuing authority printed the reference on the document, and a tier the corpus does not hold is shown as a stated gap rather than closed.

Each policy opens on one page carrying all three: its own text down to the paragraph, the authority above it, and the version history behind it. A reference in that text is a link to the policy it names, not a string.

Semper Admin is the public identity of GOATS, the Generalized Orders Administrative Tools programme. The programme is scoped to the United States Government; the Marine Corps and the Department of the Navy are the first use case and the proof of concept.

  • Authority chains - 5 policy spines, statute to message
  • Connections - the whole network, 54 documents and 351 cited references
  • Version history - the MOS Manual across 25 years and four identifier schemes
  • Verification - what a person has confirmed, what is still unread, and how a value is admitted
