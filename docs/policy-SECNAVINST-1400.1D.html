p-1 · 1. Purpose. To establish the officer competitive categories

1. Purpose. To establish the officer competitive categories within the United States Navy (USN) and United States Marine Corps (USMC) for officers on the Active-Duty List (ADL) under references (a) and (b), and criteria to assess the need for new competitive categories. This instruction is a complete revision in accordance with references (a) through (g) and should be reviewed in its entirety.
2. Cancellation. SECNAVINST 1400.1C.

This instruction applies to the Office of the Secretary of the Navy, the Chief of Naval Operations, the Commandant of the Marine Corps, and all USN and USMC installations, commands, activities, field offices, and all other organizational entities within the Department of the Navy (DON).

p-4 · 4. Policy. DON policy is to establish officer competitive

4. Policy. DON policy is to establish officer competitive categories to provide for separate promotion consideration and career development of groups of officers with related education, training, skills, and experience needed to meet mission objectives of the Navy or USMC and to ensure maximum flexibility of officer management. Each officer on the ADL within the USN SECNAVINST 1400.1D 08 Apr 2025 2 and the USMC will be appointed to a competitive category for these purposes. New competitive categories will be established when needed to meet the mission objective of the services. It is therefore intended that a periodic review of existing competitive categories will be made. Changes to the competitive categories will be made, based on the criteria in this instruction, only after careful consideration of the long-term management needs of the service. If changes occur and enclosures (1) and (2) of this instruction have not been updated, each service annual officer promotion plan will publish promotion zones for the current competitive categories as stipulated by reference (c).

p-5 · 5. Criteria for New Competitive Categories. A separate

5. Criteria for New Competitive Categories. A separate competitive category for promotion will be considered only when the following criteria are met:
a. The specialized education, training, or experience and often relatively narrow utilization of a group of officers makes it impossible for them to compete for promotion on an equitable basis with other officers having more generalized experience.
b. It is necessary to protect a substantial investment in education, training, or experience by ensuring equitable promotion opportunity when that education, training, or experience will be utilized within a narrow career field.
c. The specialized community can be managed as a separate career field in such a manner as to ensure the most efficient use of the unique resources in the various officer grades.
d. The specialized community will be large enough to sustain a career force in the grades O4 through O6, generally within the promotion guidelines of reference (c).

6. Competitive Categories. The competitive categories for ADL officers are established in enclosures (1) and (2). Each officer on the ADL shall initially be assigned to the competitive category for which recruited. Changes to an officer's competitive category and specialty shall be made per service regulation.

p-7 · 7. Request to Establish or Disestablish a Competitive Category.

7. Request to Establish or Disestablish a Competitive Category. The Deputy Chief of Naval Operations (Personnel, Manpower and Training) (DCNO (PMT)) and the Deputy Commandant for Manpower and Reserve Affairs (DC (M&RA)) may submit a request to the SECNAVINST 1400.1D 08 Apr 2025 3 Assistant Secretary of the Navy (Manpower and Reserve Affairs) (ASN (M&RA)) to establish or disestablish a competitive category when the guidelines in paragraphs 4 and 5 of this instruction are met. The requests must include:
a. A description of career area or career fields to be established or disestablished as a separate competitive category.
b. A discussion of the unique use of the proposed competitive category compared with other fields not established as separate competitive categories.
c. A projection of proposed promotion outlook (promotion opportunity and timing) for grades O4 through O6 for five years. If the requested category will take time to increase to proposed full strength, an estimate of the ability to attain and sustain the promotion guidelines in reference (e) is required.
d. Complete justification for establishing or disestablishing the competitive category. Include an evaluation of the proposal and how it will enhance the management of the resource. Evaluate the advantages and disadvantages in terms of officer accession and retention for the proposed category and the category(ies) from which separated or into which merged.

p-8 · 8. Responsibilities

8. Responsibilities
a. The ASN (M&RA) is responsible for overall supervision of the competitive category designation system under this instruction.
b. The DCNO (PMT) and the DC (M&RA) shall periodically review and update the competitive categories in their respective services.

9. Internal Controls. In accordance with reference (d), the establishment and use of internal controls and accounting procedures are mandated to ensure: effectiveness and efficiency of operations, reliability of financial reporting, and compliance with applicable laws and regulations. Additionally, as part of the annual Manager’s Internal Control Program report, the Navy and USMC will provide the ASN (M&RA) with copies of the sections of their reports that are relevant to officer SECNAVINST 1400.1D 08 Apr 2025 4 competitive categories for the ADL. The reports will include summary descriptions of internal controls used, their sufficiency, and any identified weaknesses or deficiencies.
