Verification

Nothing here is trusted because a machine produced it. A value is used only after a named person has opened the issuing authority's copy and confirmed it, and that reading is bound to the exact content confirmed. If the content later changes, the reading stops applying and the page says so.

QUORUM_SHORT 4 · UNVERIFIED 1 · VERIFIED 2

Quorum deviation in force since 2026-08-08. rule claims require 1 independent reading(s) rather than 2. Reason: One verifier exists. A two-person rule with one available person produces either permanent QUORUM_SHORT or invented second identities, and an invented identity is far worse than an honest quorum of one. The deviation is recorded here and reported by tools/verify_status.py on every run rather than hidden in a default.

The process

Steps 4 and 5 are the ones a person does. Everything else is mechanical.

  • 1
    Issuance
    The issuing authority publishes. Their copy governs, always.
  • 2
    Encode
    A provision or a rule value is recorded with the paragraph it was read from.
  • 3
    UNVERIFIED
    Everything machine-produced enters here. Nothing is assumed correct.
  • 4
    A human reads
    A named person opens the authority's copy and compares. This step cannot be automated.
  • 5
    Attest
    The reading is recorded against a hash of the exact content confirmed.
  • 6
    VERIFIED
    Derived, never stored. If the content changes, the reading stops applying.
  • 7
    Used
    Only verified values are compared or published. Unverified ones are withheld and say so.

What the states mean

StateMeaning
VERIFIEDA named person read the issuing authority's copy and confirmed this.
QUORUM SHORTRead and confirmed, but short of the independent readings this kind of claim requires.
INVALIDATEDConfirmed once, then the content changed. The earlier reading no longer applies.
REJECTEDA person read the source and says the encoding is wrong. A found defect.
UNABLEA person tried and could not, usually because the source could not be obtained.
UNVERIFIEDNobody has read the source against this record.

Step 2 · The reading

Open the issuing authority's copy. Find the cited paragraph. Confirm it states this value, in these units, at that paragraph and not a neighbouring one.

Do not confirm from the note on the record, from another tier, or from memory. If the paragraph says something else, answer rejected - a found defect is worth more than a confirmation.

Step 4 · Admitting it

Put the file in verification/incoming/ and a maintainer runs:

python tools/attest.py --ingest

That step recomputes the hash from the corpus and compares it with the one your browser saw. If they differ, the record changed after you read it and the file is refused - the reading has to happen again. Nothing is repaired silently.

Then bash build.sh regenerates this page and every page that uses the value.

Validate a claim

This page cannot write to the library. It produces an attestation file that a maintainer admits with python tools/attest.py --ingest, which recomputes the hash from the corpus rather than trusting the one in the file. If they differ, the file is refused - that means the record changed after you read it, and the reading has to happen again.

Open the issuing authority's copy first. Do not confirm from this page, from a note, or from memory. The link is on the claim.
