About

Semper Admin is the public identity of GOATS, the Generalized Orders Administrative Tools programme. This site is the programme's proof of concept: Marine Corps and Department of the Navy policy recast as structured data, the way Congress publishes statute.

What this is not

  • Not an official reference. This is not a Marine Corps, Department of the Navy, or Department of Defense site, and it holds no Authority to Operate. It is maintained on a personal basis. The issuing authority's copy of every document governs, always.
  • Not a decision. Nothing here determines a Marine's entitlement. Where a value is computed it is a worksheet with a citation on every line, and a person signs.
  • Not complete. {{docs}} documents, chosen because their authority chains are deep enough to test the model. The whole-of-government scope in the programme statement is aspiration, and is labelled as such wherever it appears.
  • Not automated judgement. Every value a machine extracted is marked unverified until a named person has read the issuing authority's copy and confirmed it. Unverified values are withheld from every comparison.

Where the documents come from

Public issuances published by the issuing authority: marines.mil, the DoD Directives Division, and the Office of the Law Revision Counsel for the U.S. Code. Documents carrying a limited distribution statement are excluded from the site, the search index, and the exports. Contact details printed in the issuances are withheld from every published copy.

Terms

The tooling, the schema, and the identifier register are released under the MIT licence. The text of United States Government issuances carries no copyright and is reproduced as published. The Semper Admin mark is excluded from the licence. The full licence and notice are in the repository at github.com/SemperAdmin/policy-as-data.

Accessibility

Pages target the revised Section 508 standards (WCAG 2.0 AA). The accessibility statement records what is provided and how to report a barrier.

Feedback

Corrections and questions: feedback form. A correction to an encoded value goes through the verification process, never straight into the data.
