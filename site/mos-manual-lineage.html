What changed this year

A provision-level comparison of the Introduction to the two most recent editions, NAVMC 1200.1L (FY26) and NAVMC 1200.1M (FY27) - parsed to paragraphs and diffed by citation.

NAVMC 1200.1L (FY26) → NAVMC 1200.1M (FY27)

  • 2. Reporting authority changed: “CG, TECOM will submit a report of changes” became “DC, T&E will submit”.
  • 3.a. Approval authority changed: “No change will be accepted by CG, TECOM” became “accepted by DC, T&E”.
  • 4.b. MOS renamed: 8040 “Colonel Logistician” became “Colonel Sustainment.”
  • 2.b.(1) Approval chain refined: “CG TECOM/training command approved POI” became “CG TECOM/TECOM Major Subordinate Command CG approved POI.”

17 paragraphs unchanged.

The FY27 edition carries a single coherent change: Training and Education Command's authority moved to the Deputy Commandant, Training and Education - TECOM becomes DC, T&E throughout - and one MOS was renamed from Logistician to Sustainment. The diff surfaced this automatically from two 970-page PDFs.
