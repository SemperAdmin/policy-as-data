## Why it earns a row

The project uses the word ontology in `schema/authority-ontology.ttl`, in
`NAMESPACES.md`, and in conversation with reviewers who hold the philosophical
sense of the word. Those two senses are not the same, and an outside reviewer
holding the philosophical sense reads the project's usage as overreach. This
entry is the citation to settle that, and the SEP archival URL is stable per
edition, which most web citations in `Web_resources.csv` are not.

Cite the edition-pinned archive URL, never the bare entry URL. The bare URL
tracks the current edition and silently changes under a citation.
